# Ground Rules
1. ## Work Norms: How will work be distributed?
   Who will set deadlines? The professor will set the deadlines, although I think it best to ensure that all work is largely completed in advance of the deadline.

   What happens if someone doesn't follow through on his/her commitment (for example, misses a deadline)? If the work is not completed within a reasonable timeframe relative to the deadline, then, unless someone volunteers to do it, I'll do it. If everyone forgets about the assignment or the deadline, then we submit for partial credit or take the loss.

   How will the work be reviewed? Those who choose to review work will raise any potential concerns with its efficacy.

   What happens if people have different opinions about the quality of the work? As long as the work achieves it's main purpose, the specific content of the work itself is secondary. If two memebers have conflicting opinions, and they cannot compromise, then it is up to the group as a whole to make a decision.

   What happens if people have different work habits? Facilitating flexible environment is important in accommodating different work habits.
