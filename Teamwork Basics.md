# Ground Rules
1. ## Work Norms: How will work be distributed?
   Who will set deadlines? The professor will set the deadlines, although the group consensus is that it is best to ensure that all work is largely completed in advance of the deadline, although to what extent is undecided.

   What happens if someone doesn't follow through on his/her commitment (for example, misses a deadline)? If the work is not completed within a reasonable timeframe relative to the deadline, then there exists at least one of us who will finish the work last minute. If everyone forgets about the assignment or the deadline, then we submit for partial credit or take the loss.

   How will the work be reviewed? We conduct a suitability analysis and anyone who chooses to review work will raise any potential concerns with its efficacy.

   What happens if people have different opinions about the quality of the work? As long as the work achieves it's main purpose, the specific content of the work itself is secondary. If two members have conflicting opinions, and they cannot compromise, then it is up to the group as a whole to mediate and make a decision.

   What happens if people have different work habits? Facilitating a flexible environment is important in accommodating different work habits, but we should be able to largely work independently.

   What happens if people are late to a meeting?

   What happens if a group member misses a meeting?

   What if he/ she misses several meetings?
