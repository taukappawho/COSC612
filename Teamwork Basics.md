# Ground Rules
1. ## Work Norms: How will work be distributed?
   #### Who will set deadlines?
    The professor will set the deadlines, although the group consensus is that it is best to ensure that all work is largely completed in advance of the deadline, although to what extent is undecided.

   #### What happens if someone doesn't follow through on his/her commitment (for example, misses a deadline)?
    If the work is not completed within a reasonable timeframe relative to the deadline, then there exists at least one of us who will finish the work last minute. If everyone forgets about the assignment or the deadline, then we submit for partial credit or take the loss.

   #### How will the work be reviewed?
    We conduct a suitability analysis. Anyone who chooses to review work will raise any potential concerns with its efficacy.

   #### What happens if people have different opinions about the quality of the work?
    As long as the work achieves it's main purpose, the specific content of the work itself is secondary. If two members have conflicting opinions, and they cannot compromise, then it is up to the group as a whole to mediate and make a decision.

   #### What happens if people have different work habits?
    Facilitating a flexible environment is important in accommodating different work habits, but we should be able to largely work independently.
   
2. ## Facilitator Norms:
   #### Will you use a facilitator?
      Yes.

   #### How will the facilitator be chosen?
     Whoever volunteers in the pool of remaining individuals.
  
     #### Will you rotate the position?
     Yes. The pattern of facilitator selection will be repeated once all members have been the facilitator.
   
     #### What are the responsibilities of the facilitator?
      • To communicate with the professor
      • Complete any uncompleted work
      • Submit the documents for the sprint
      • Focus the team on the task (both short term and long term)
      • Get participation from all team members
      • Keep the team to its agreed-upon time frame (both short term and long term)
      • Suggest alternative procedures when the team is stalled
      • Help team members confront problems
      • Summarize and clarify the team’s decisions
   
4. ## Communication Norms:
   #### When should communication takes place and through what medium (e.g., do some people prefer to communicate through e-mail while others would rather talk on the phone)?
    We have Discord setup, and questions can be asked at any time. Thus far it has worked well. Questions can be asked, answered, files shared, screenshots shared. All without everyone needing to be present simultaneously.

5. ## Meeting Norms:
   #### What is everyone's schedule?
   
      Everyone agreed that Tuesday 3:00 pm is fine.
      Alternatively, Friday was good as well.

   #### Should one person be responsible for coordinating meetings? Do people have a preference for when meetings are held? Where is a good place to hold meetings?
   Thus far, communicating via Discord has been sufficient. If an ad hoc meeting is necessary, it can be arranged via Discord.

     #### What happens if people are late to a meeting?
      No consequences.
     #### What happens if a group member misses a meeting?
      It is their responsibility to find out what was discussed during the meeting. Thus far, everything has been by text on Discord, all they would have to do is read the chat.
    ####  What if he/ she misses several meetings?
    They would have to read more lines of chat
 
6. ## Consideration Norms:
   #### Can people eat at meetings?
   sure
   #### Smoke cigarettes?
   certainly
   #### Smoke other stuff?
   only if they bring enough to share

   #### What happens if someone is dominating the discussion?
   follow the suggestions on how to deal with difficult individuals. politely steer the conversation to others, and perhaps discreetly and privately address the issue with the individual in a non-confrontational manner.

   #### How can norms be changed if someone is not comfortable with what is going on in the team?
   They can state they're not comfortable with a particular norm in Discord. They can ask for a group meeting to discuss what could be different/how things could be handled differently. They could contact the professor who is always available and willing to help in such situations.
